# Techshipped Studio

**Explore. Understand. Build.**

Techshipped Studio is a technology education and knowledge-sharing channel dedicated to making complex technologies easier to understand and apply.

We explore Artificial Intelligence, Generative AI, Agentic AI, software engineering, cloud and data platforms, developer tools, and emerging technology trends. Through technical deep dives, practical demonstrations and hands-on tutorials, our goal is to bridge the gap between understanding technology and applying it in the real world.

## 📺 What you'll find here

- AI, GenAI and Agentic AI concepts and applications
- Software engineering practices, architectures and tools
- Data engineering and modern cloud platforms
- Hands-on technical tutorials and demonstrations
- Emerging technology insights and perspectives
